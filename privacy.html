Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you visit guestanchor.com or contact Muhammad Nabeel Khan about GuestAnchor services, including guest posting, blogger outreach, editorial link building, niche edits, and publisher research.

1. Who we are

GuestAnchor is the professional website of Muhammad Nabeel Khan. For privacy questions or requests, contact nabeelkhan@guestanchor.com.

2. Information we may receive

Depending on how you interact with the website, we may receive information that you choose to provide, such as:

  • Your name, business name, email address, and other contact details.
  • Your company or website address, target URL, target country, niche, requested service, and project requirements.
  • Information about the number or type of placements requested, preferred publishers, content requirements, anchor text, and messages you send.
  • Correspondence and details you share when communicating with us by email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or another agreed channel.

Some technical information may also be recorded when you visit the website, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, referring page, and visit date or time. The information collected depends on the website hosting, security, and analytics services in use.

3. How we use information

We may use relevant information to:

  • Respond to questions, enquiries, and quotation requests.
  • Understand your requirements and assess whether a requested service is suitable.
  • Research relevant publishers and coordinate agreed guest posting, outreach, content, or link-placement work.
  • Communicate about project progress, revisions, delivery, and support.
  • Maintain business records, protect the website, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Understand general website traffic and improve website content and performance.

4. Analytics and cookies

GuestAnchor uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content and performance. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers and process technical information such as pages visited, device or browser details, and usage data.

Google explains how Analytics collects and processes information in its Safeguarding your data guidance and its Privacy Disclosures Policy. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect certain website features.

5. When information may be shared

We do not sell your personal contact information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, for example:

  • With service providers that help host, secure, maintain, or measure the performance of the website.
  • With relevant publishers, website owners, or editors when sharing specific project details is necessary to carry out a service you have requested or approved.
  • Where disclosure is required by law, a valid legal request, or to protect rights, safety, and the security of the website.

We aim to limit shared information to what is reasonably relevant to the particular purpose. We do not authorize service providers to use information shared for a project for their unrelated marketing.

6. International processing

GuestAnchor may work with clients and publishers in different countries. Depending on the service providers or publisher involved, relevant information may be processed or accessed outside your country. Where this happens, we aim to use information only as needed for the stated business purpose and to take reasonable steps to protect it.

7. Data retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, protect the website, or meet legal requirements. Retention periods may vary according to the type and purpose of the information.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask for deletion where appropriate. You may also object to or ask us to restrict certain uses of your information where applicable law provides that right. We may need to retain some information for legitimate business, security, or legal reasons.

To make a privacy request, email nabeelkhan@guestanchor.com and describe your request. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the request relates to you.

9. Third-party websites

This website may link to third-party websites or services. Those websites operate under their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of websites that we do not control. Please review their policies before sharing information with them.

10. Children’s privacy

GuestAnchor’s services are intended for businesses, website owners, and professionals, not children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when our services, website practices, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
